# FANDOMWIRE ERA-2 PLAYBOOK — Post-Spam-Update (Aug 2026)

Synthesized from a 6-dimension adversarial mining run: BEFORE corpus n=1,433 competitor titles / 41,352 all rows; AFTER corpus n=524 / 19,796. Every number below is a CONFIRMED (or corrected) recompute — nothing invented. Era-1 rules: `FW-MICRO-PLAYBOOK.md` (S1–S24). Context: FW Discover -59% after the update; competitor set held its formula almost entirely intact.

**Headline of the analysis: the update did NOT reprice the competitor title formula.** Structure, length, listicles, superlatives, "Officially" — all stable. What moved is small and thematic: less tease/announcement framing (confirms/reveals down ~19-28% rel.), fewer question/quote/speculation titles (already rare → near zero), MORE time-anchor/retro framing (+38%) and MORE streaming-platform name-drops (+30% rel., significant).

## 4. TEAM MEMO (for Akash's writers)

1. Ghabrao mat — formula zinda hai. Listicles, "Officially", long titles, superlatives: sab stable, koi penalty nahi.
2. "X Is Officially..." likhte raho — 17% competitor titles mein hai, update ke baad bhi.
3. "Confirms/Reveals/Teases" thoda kam karo — status batao, tease mat karo. Announcement-bait down hai.
4. Naya winner: time anchors. "25 Years Later, [Franchise]..." type framing +38% up — har hafte kuch retro/anniversary angles banao.
5. Netflix/HBO ka naam title mein daalo jab relevant ho — platform name-drops significantly up (Netflix 5%→8%).
6. Questions band: "Why/How/What..." openers aur "?" — competitors ne almost zero kar diya. Declarative hi likho.
7. Quotes bhi band — quoted-phrase headlines competitors ne zero kar diye.
8. Listicle counts: 5 ya 8 try karo, 10 kam ("10 best" collapsed, 5 ab #1). ", Ranked" safe hai.
9. "Every X..." format skip karo — winners ne half se zyada kaat diya.
10. Length mat ghatao: 13+ words normal hai, short titles (≤8 words) aur gir gaye. Em-dash kabhi nahi.
