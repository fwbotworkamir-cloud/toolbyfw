Title Rules by the Numbers

📏 Rule 1: 11.7 words, 70.1 characters

The average Discover entertainment title is exactly 11.7 words. Titles over 90 characters get truncated in the feed. Stay between 12-16 words.

➖ Rule 2: Use the emdash (—) — 60.3% do

The most common title structure separates the hook from context with a dash or emdash. This is THE Discover format.

"Dakota Johnson stuns as Marilyn Monroe in new film — first trailer drops"

🆕 Rule 3: "New" is the #1 word — 13.9% of titles

Discover's core value proposition is showing users things they haven't seen. "New" directly signals this.

"Netflix reveals new season 3 premiere date for hit drama"

🔢 Rule 4: Numbers embedded, not leading — 36.9% contain, only 6.4% start

Numbers add specificity ("$285M opening weekend") but starting with them ("10 Ways to...") underperforms.

"'Spider-Man: Brand New Day' swings to $285M opening weekend — a franchise record"

❓ Rule 5: Avoid questions — only 5.3% make the cut

Discover rewards answers, not questions. Convert "Why did X happen?" into "X happened because Y — here's what it means."
